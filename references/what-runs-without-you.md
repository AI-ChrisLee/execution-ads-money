# What runs without you, and what does not

Read this before telling a founder anything about overnight.

## 1. While the laptop is shut

- **The cap holds**, once a campaign is on. `spend_cap` is, in Meta's words, "the maximum amount the
  campaign can spend". Say it that way: the most this campaign can spend. Never a time of night it
  stops.
- **This agent does nothing.** It reads the account and writes the card only when the founder types.

## 2. Lines this agent never says

- "Growing while you sleep" or "working overnight" about the agent. Only the cap holds overnight.
- "Meta stops it at 3am." Say "the most this campaign can spend".

## 3. What is never built

- **No schedule.** No cron, no cloud routine, no scheduled task.
- **No browser on Ads Manager.** Meta's terms do not allow automated access without its
  permission. The connector is the way Meta built for this.
